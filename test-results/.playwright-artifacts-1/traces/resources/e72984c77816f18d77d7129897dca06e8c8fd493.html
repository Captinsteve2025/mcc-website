Meet Your Crew

The professionals who turn a charter into a personal experience — introduced with the details they have confirmed for this yacht.

Hans Jürgen Arndt

Crew member

Raised along the South African coast, Hans developed a love for the ocean through fishing, surfing, diving and lifesaving. By the age of 16, he was already skippering boats, beginning a journey that has grown into 20 years of seagoing experience, including 10 years of professional service aboard private and commercial sailing and motor vessels. After studying Industrial Operations Management in Industrial Engineering, Hans began a corporate career before deciding to follow his passion for the sea. His sailing adventures have taken him from the waters of South Africa aboard on a Farr 38 to transatlantic catamaran crossings from Cape Town to the Caribbean onto regattas aboard an 80-foot Swann in the United states and Caribbean. He later spent two years captaining an expedition yacht through the South Pacific, exploring remote islands and spectacular cruising grounds while discovering new dive locations. A qualified dive instructor with hands-on engineering experience, Hans brings both adventure and practical expertise to life aboard Endless Horizon. He loves exploring secluded anchorages, inspiring guests to try all watersports and adventures, and sharing his enthusiasm for the ocean. Hans also enjoys setting the scene and creating a great atmosphere on board, whether it’s a day filled with adventure or a relaxed evening at anchor. Approachable and attentive, he takes pride in the details that make each charter feel special, ensuring guests feel safe, relaxed and free to enjoy every moment of their time aboard.

Anthony Fraser

Crew member

Anthony has spent much of his life on or around the water. His love of boating, engineering and adventure led him to a career at sea, where he enjoys both running a yacht and keeping everything aboard working as it should. A commercially endorsed RYA Yachtmaster and experienced marine engineer, Anthony has worked on a range of vessels, from high-performance motor yachts to luxury superyachts, including Sunreef catamarans. Practical and hands-on, he is equally at home at the helm or in the engine room. With a meticulous approach to maintenance and a deep understanding of yacht systems, he takes pride in keeping the yacht well maintained, prepared and ready for the next adventure. Outside work, Anthony is a keen spearfisherman and former chairman of the British Spearfishing Association. Years spent diving at home in south-west England and around the world, have given him a lasting appreciation for marine life and the ocean. He loves sharing that enthusiasm with guests, whether snorkelling a reef, exploring the coastline or helping someone feel more confident in the water. At 6'6", Anthony is easy to spot on deck. His easygoing nature, quick wit and British sense of humour help guests settle into life aboard Endless Horizon. He loves creating a relaxed, welcoming atmosphere where guests can feel completely at home. For Anthony, the perfect charter combines exceptional service, unforgettable experiences and just the right amount of adventure.

British

Heather Arndt

Crew member

With 10 years of experience in private and charter yachting, Heather brings together a passion for exceptional cuisine, global flavors and life on the ocean. Her culinary style blends classical training, creative flair and inspiration gathered from more than 27,000 nautical miles of travel. Her culinary journey began at a farm-to-table bakery on Martha’s Vineyard, followed by formal training at the Institute of Culinary Education in New York City and a stage at Jean-Georges’ Mark Restaurant. Her entrepreneurial spirit later inspired her to establish an artisan chocolate business from her home in Maine, supplying local businesses and corporate clients with handcrafted creations. Drawn to the ocean and an adventurous lifestyle, Heather found a natural home in yachting. Over the past decade, she has worked aboard a variety of private and charter vessels, continually developing her craft through international culinary courses in Palma, England, the South of France and Bangkok. Her cooking blends classical foundations with global influences, from beautifully executed comfort-food classics to elegant fine dining and creative culinary fusions. With a particular passion for pastry, artisan breads and handcrafted specialties, she enjoys creating memorable dining experiences inspired by the destinations she visits. Her travels have taken her through the Panama Canal, across the South Pacific, to New Zealand, throughout Europe and the Bahamas, and along the eastern coast of the United States. These experiences have deepened her appreciation for local cultures, regional ingredients and the flavors that make each destination unique. Beyond the galley, she enjoys diving as a PADI instructor, surfing, hiking, fitness and water sports. Always curious and eager to explore, she loves discovering new cultures and culinary traditions wherever her travels take her. For Heather, great food is about creating connections, sharing experiences and adding something special to every journey.

Ella Hunter-Campbell

Crew member

Ella’s connection to the sea began long before her professional yachting career. Living by the sea in the south west of England, and sailing with her grandfather from a young age on her family’s Swan 46, Troubadour, she developed a love of life on the water through coastal cruising, offshore passages and competitive racing. While studying at University College London, she also competed with the university sailing club, combining her studies with weekends on the racecourse. After graduating with a First Class Master’s degree in Earth Science, Ella completed her superyacht training at UKSA and gained experience aboard private motor yachts in the Mediterranean, including a newly delivered Sunreef 100. Comfortable across both deck and interior, she brings a practical approach, an eye for detail and a willingness to turn her hand to whatever the day brings. Her background in hospitality spans local restaurants, cocktail bars, weddings and events. From preparing a favourite drink to adding thoughtful finishing touches before guests arrive, Ella enjoys the details that help people feel welcome and looked after. Aboard Endless Horizon, she brings that same care to creating a relaxed and memorable charter experience. Away from work, Ella enjoys yoga, fitness, photography, art and exploring new places. Her adventurous spirit has taken her to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro on a charity trek and to Ecuador for community and environmental projects. With a keen interest in the natural world and a longstanding commitment to volunteering, she brings curiosity, warmth and a love of adventure to life on board.

Lizé Viljoen

Crew member

Growing up spending time on the ocean and fishing with her father, Lizé developed natural sea legs and a deep connection to the water from an early age. Her passion for the underwater world led her to become a PADI Dive Instructor, blending her love for adventure with professional maritime expertise. Before entering the superyacht industry, Lizé spent three years working in hospitality, honing her customer service skills and attention to detail. Transitioning to yachting, she spent five months aboard Ellen’s Dream as a stewardess, playing a hands-on role in setting up the new build vessel. During her time onboard, she gained invaluable deck and seamanship experience participating in a 2,500-nautical-mile ocean crossing from Poland to Gibraltar. Lizé brings an adaptable, hardworking, and team-oriented approach to life at sea. Known for her flexibility and strong work ethic, she thrives both on deck and in the interior, ensuring smooth operations and an exceptional guest experience. Away from yachting, Lizé stays active and true to her coastal roots. She is happiest around the water, whether diving, fitness training, fishing, hiking, or simply enjoying the ocean.

Accommodations Details
Endless Horizon features four elegantly furnished cabins for guests. The main cabin is a luxurious retreat, complete with a king-sized bed, an expansive bathroom, a snug sofa, and ample closet space. Three more double cabins, each featuring a oversize queen-size bed with walk-around access, ample windows, and a fully furnished bathroom. Every cabin has a flat-screen TV and USB outlets for convenient charging of electronic gadgets. The crew quarters are conveniently situated with independent access at the back of the main salon.
